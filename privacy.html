Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Riznefy provides digital marketing services to clients across the US, UK, Europe, UAE, KSA, Qatar, and Bangladesh. This policy explains what information we collect when you visit our website or work with us, how we use it, and the choices you have.

01

Information We Collect

We collect information in a few different ways, depending on how you interact with us:

  • Information you give us directly — your name, WhatsApp number, email, brand/website details, and any message content when you submit our contact form, message us on WhatsApp, or email us.
  • Information collected automatically — your browser type, device type, approximate location (city/country level), pages visited, and how you arrived at our site, collected through analytics tools described below.
  • Information from working together — if you become a client, we may collect additional business information (ad account access, website analytics, product/pricing details) necessary to deliver our services, governed by a separate service agreement.
02

How We Use It

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to inquiries submitted through our contact form, WhatsApp, or email
  • Understand how visitors use our website, so we can improve it
  • Deliver, manage, and report on services for clients we work with
  • Send updates related to an active engagement (never unsolicited marketing to visitors who haven't reached out)

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

03

Cookies & Tracking

Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies, which generally fall into these categories:

  • Essential cookies — required for the site to function correctly (e.g., remembering your cookie preferences, keeping the mobile menu and forms working properly). These cannot be disabled without affecting basic site functionality.
  • Analytics cookies — help us understand how visitors use the site, via Google Analytics (GA4), so we can see aggregate traffic patterns and improve content and navigation.
  • Marketing cookies — help us measure the effectiveness of our own ad campaigns, via Meta Pixel, and understand which content resonates with visitors who arrived from Meta platforms (Facebook/Instagram).
  • Third-party cookies — some pages may load resources (such as fonts or embedded content) from third-party services, which may set their own cookies subject to their respective privacy policies.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings at any time. Disabling non-essential cookies may affect some website functionality but will not prevent you from browsing the site or contacting us.

04

Sharing Your Information

We share information only in these limited circumstances:

  • Service providers — tools we rely on to run our business (CRM, analytics, communication platforms) may process your data on our behalf, under their own privacy and security obligations.
  • Legal requirements — if required to do so by law or in response to a valid legal request.
  • Business transfers — in the event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction.

We do not share your information with third parties for their own independent marketing purposes.

05

Your Rights & Choices

Depending on where you're located, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information. To exercise any of these rights, contact us at riznefy@gmail.com or through WhatsApp, and we'll respond within a reasonable timeframe.

You can also opt out of non-essential communications from us at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or simply letting us know.

06

Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information we collect from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

07

Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for business owners and marketing professionals, not children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under the age of 16. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected such information, we will take steps to delete it.

08

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal, operational, or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Continued use of our website after changes are posted means you accept the updated policy.

09

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, reach out:

  • Email: riznefy@gmail.com
  • WhatsApp: +880 1712-650589

This Privacy Policy is provided as a general reference for how Riznefy handles information and is not a substitute for tailored legal advice. If your jurisdiction has specific data protection requirements applicable to your use of this site, we recommend consulting a qualified legal professional.
